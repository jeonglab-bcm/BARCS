BARCS evidence graph: developmental biology

15 developmental-biology screens from 11 papers (cell fate, differentiation, pluripotency, lineage specification, X inactivation, haematopoiesis), reanalyzed with BARCS, every tested gene kept, joined to STRING v12.5. The pathway map shows which interacting genes are hit together in screens from different papers. The other views open the evidence behind any screen or gene. The full graph of every screen is on the main evidence-graph page; each screen's own reanalysis is on the BARCS reanalysis site.

Each card is one module. The small network shows which of its genes interact and how many papers hit each pair together. The grid lists every screen that hits the module, with ▲ where guides were enriched and ▼ where they were depleted, in the contrast as oriented; what that means for the phenotype is not curated yet. Hover a screen to find it in other cards, click it to open its network.

    Where the calls are

    Each dot is one contrast. Contrasts that call more than 10% of what they test hold most of the corpus's calls; the shaded region is what cross-screen analyses use. Select a dot to open its network.
